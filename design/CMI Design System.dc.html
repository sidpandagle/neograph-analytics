Custom Market Insights

A confident, data-forward system: one vivid blue, one soft sky, generous white panels on a cool grey desk. Built for research reports, dashboards and marketing pages.

Color

Primary blue carries all action and emphasis. Sky is for data surfaces and illustration. Ink is reserved for footers and dark cards.

Layout rules

Panels on a desk

Every section is a 24px-radius panel on #EDF1F9, max width 1240px, 20px between panels.

Alternate weight

Blue and white panels alternate; no more than two blue panels in a row, ink only for the footer.

Numbers lead

Market sizes and CAGR set in Outfit 700; the growth figure always gets the primary blue.
