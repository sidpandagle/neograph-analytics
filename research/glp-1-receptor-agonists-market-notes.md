## The spread

**$2.6B to $62.86B at 2025 — a 24.2x spread.** Excluding the Future Market
Insights outlier, $28.7B to $62.86B, still **2.19x**.

This is a market where the spread cannot be excused as a definition problem,
because the definition is unusually clean. "GLP-1 receptor agonist" is a
pharmacological class with a countable, approved, branded membership, and two
companies hold essentially all of it — and both report brand-level revenue in
audited filings. There is no scope ambiguity to hide behind. The spread is a
measurement failure.

## Grounding arithmetic

This is the rare market where the grounding calculation is not a plausibility
test against a proxy register. It is the thing itself: disclosed brand revenue.

**Novo Nordisk, FY2025, from the Q4 2025 Form 6-K sales tables (DKK million):**

| Brand | FY2025 |
|---|---|
| Ozempic (semaglutide, injectable, T2D) | 127,089 |
| Rybelsus (oral semaglutide) | 22,093 |
| Victoza (liraglutide) | 3,020 |
| **GLP-1 diabetes subtotal** | **152,202** |
| Wegovy (semaglutide, obesity) | 79,106 |
| Saxenda (liraglutide, obesity) | 3,241 |
| **GLP-1 total** | **234,549** |
| Total company net sales (all products) | 309,064 |

Novo's own FY2025 reporting describes GLP-1 medicines at DKK 234.5bn, up 14% —
which matches the sum above.

At the 2025 average rate disclosed in the same filing, **DKK 6.62 per USD**:
234,549 / 6.62 = **USD 35,430m**.

**Eli Lilly, FY2025, from the Q4 2025 results release ($ million):**

| Brand | FY2025 | FY2024 | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mounjaro (tirzepatide, T2D) | 22,965 | 11,540 | +99% |
| Zepbound (tirzepatide, obesity) | 13,542 | 4,926 | +175% |
| **Tirzepatide subtotal** | **36,507** | | |
| Trulicity (dulaglutide) | not found for FY2025 (Q4 alone $1.04bn, -17%) | | |
| Total company revenue | 65,179 | 45,043 | +45% |

**Two companies, FY2025 disclosed GLP-1 revenue: 35,430 + 36,507 = USD
71,937m, or $71.94 billion.**

Trulicity's full year is excluded (not located), as are Sanofi's and
AstraZeneca's legacy exenatide-class products and all Chinese domestic
semaglutide. So **$71.94B is a floor.**

Set that against the published sizes:

| Published 2025 estimate | Disclosed $71.94B as a multiple |
|---|---|
| Future Market Insights $2.6B | **27.7x** |
| IMARC $26.8B (2024) | 2.68x |
| Healthcare Foresights $28.7B | **2.51x** |
| Towards Healthcare $62.86B | **1.14x** |
| Grand View (implied 2025 ≈ $69.8B) | **1.03x — agrees within 3%** |

Three of the five published estimates are *below the audited revenue of two
companies*. Future Market Insights's $2.6B is smaller than Zepbound's Q4 alone.
Only Grand View's back-solved base survives contact with the filings.

### Where the forecasts outrun the run-rate

This is the point the brief asks for, and the arithmetic is explicit.

Apply the 17.5% rate (Towards Healthcare) to the **disclosed** $71.94B base:

> 71.94 × 1.175^6 = **$189.3B by 2032**

That requires Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly's combined GLP-1 revenue to reach
roughly **2.63x** its FY2025 level. For scale: Lilly's own guidance for FY2026
**total company** revenue, every product included, is $80-83bn. Novo's entire
FY2025 company revenue was DKK 309bn (~$46.7bn).

So a 17.5% class CAGR off a correctly measured base implies a 2032 GLP-1 class
roughly four times Lilly's current whole-company revenue. It is not arithmetically
impossible — Zepbound grew 175% in FY2025 and Mounjaro 99% — but it is a
forecast about oral semaglutide, orforglipron, manufacturing scale-up and payer
coverage decisions, not an extrapolation of a disclosed run-rate. The published
rates are mostly attached to bases 2.5x too low, where the same percentage is a
far smaller absolute claim.

**We therefore publish the lower end of the published rate range (12.1%) on the
correct base, and say so.** That gives $142.76B by 2032, and the report states
plainly what the 17.5% alternative would require.

## What we publish

- **Base:** $71.94B, the FY2025 disclosed GLP-1 brand revenue of Novo Nordisk
  and Eli Lilly, carried as the 2026 anchor without uplift (2026 revenue is not
  disclosed). Corroborated by Grand View's back-solved 2025 base of ~$69.8B.
- **CAGR:** 12.1% (Healthcare Foresights, 2026-2035) — the lowest mainstream
  published rate, paired with the highest defensible base.
- **Forecast:** $142.76B by 2032.

## Do not publish

- **Vendor market share.** Tempting here, because two companies hold almost all
  of it and both disclose — but the denominator is still a floor (Trulicity,
  exenatide-class and Chinese domestic supply are not in it), so any percentage
  would be a ratio against our own incomplete sum. Describe positions in words
  and give the absolute disclosed figures instead.
- **"North America 61.23% in 2025."** Published by a market-research house
  against a base our arithmetic contradicts. Neither Novo nor Lilly publishes a
  clean GLP-1-only geographic split, so `regional_analysis` and
  `country_analysis` stay `[]`.
- **Compounded-pharmacy and grey-market volume.** Materially large during
  shortage periods, not publicly measured. Mentioned qualitatively, not sized.
- **Trulicity FY2025.** Not located; not reconstructed from quarters.
- **Patient counts or treated-population figures.** Widely quoted, not
  publicly registered at a checkable level.
